QueueLess is engineered with privacy-by-design principles:

  • No patient personal identifiable information (PII) is written to application log files.
  • Patient phone numbers and contact details are stored pseudonymized or hashed.
  • Public queue screens display only opaque token numbers (e.g., A-102, W-204) without patient names.
  • System activity audit logs are strictly append-only.

Notice: This instance operates in Demonstration Mode with synthetic datasets only.
